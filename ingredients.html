Compare

Ingredient Comparison

Understanding the difference between key ingredients.

Vitamin C vs. Retinol

Vitamin C is a powerful antioxidant that brightens skin and protects against environmental damage. It's ideal for morning use to defend against free radicals.

Retinol is a vitamin A derivative that accelerates cell turnover and stimulates collagen. Best used at night for anti-aging and skin renewal. Together, they create a powerful skincare duo.

Hyaluronic Acid vs. Rosehip Oil

Hyaluronic Acid is a humectant that draws moisture into the skin, providing intense hydration without clogging pores. Perfect for all skin types.

Rosehip Oil is a nourishing oil rich in essential fatty acids and vitamins. It helps repair skin, fade scars, and restore the skin's natural barrier.
